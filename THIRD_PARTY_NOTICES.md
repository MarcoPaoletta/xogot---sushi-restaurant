# Third-party assets

The MIT license in `LICENSE` covers the project's own work: scenes, scripts, shaders, materials, the generated audio (`assets/audio`, produced by `tools/gen_audio.py`) and the documentation in `docs/`.

The `assets/` folder also contains third-party content that ships under its own, separate license. Each pack keeps its license file inside its folder, and those licenses apply to those files regardless of the MIT license on the rest of the repository.
